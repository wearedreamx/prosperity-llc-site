<p>It’s summer, and we Chicagoans love to get the most out of our waterfront and the warm weather. So we took to the river with Wateriders Kayak tour! After a brief lesson on the basics, we launched into the “urban canyon”. We paddled down the south branch to see if we could spot the office from the water, then headed back to the east branch before docking again. What a unique way to see downtown!</p>
