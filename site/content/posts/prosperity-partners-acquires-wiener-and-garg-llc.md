We’re proud to announce our partnership with Wiener and Garg LLC (WG)! We’re excited to have everyone in the Rockville office on the team and look forward to a rewarding and prosperous future together.

Founded in 1980, W&G provides a range of tax, consulting, and accounting services to businesses and high-net-worth individuals. Led by the firm’s partners Subhash Garg and Vikesh Bansal, W&G possesses a strategic focus on serving high-net-worth individuals and industries such as real estate and real estate construction, software development, legal services, and franchisers.

“We are delighted to officially partner with the entire W&G team and welcome them to the Prosperity Partners family,” said Jeremy Dubow, CEO and co-founder of Prosperity. “Subhash and Vikesh are both outstanding individuals, and we’re confident that our alignment on premier client service and key service offerings will make W&G a great addition to the Prosperity platform.”

Consistent with Prosperity’s people-first focus, all W&G employees will become part of Prosperity’s Employee Purpose Plan, a broad-based employee ownership program. As an essential aspect of each Unity Partners’ investment, the employee ownership plan allows all employees to benefit from the company’s success.

“It’s a true honor to launch this partnership and we look forward to leveraging our firm’s decades of experience to help strengthen and deliver high-quality service to all Prosperity clients,” said Subhash and Vikesh. “Throughout our meetings with Jeremy and the Prosperity group, their people-first, team-oriented culture and extensive expertise within the tax and accounting industry resonated with us, and we’re excited to be joining such a well-established organization.”
