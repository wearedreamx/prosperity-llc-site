This July, our Chicago team partnered with the Lincoln Park Conservancy for a day of service, helping spread mulch around park trees and pick up litter throughout the park.

Despite the summer heat, our team worked side by side to help care for one of Chicago’s treasured green spaces while enjoying time together outside the office. Opportunities like these reflect Prosperity’s people-first culture and our commitment to making a positive impact in the communities where we live and work.
