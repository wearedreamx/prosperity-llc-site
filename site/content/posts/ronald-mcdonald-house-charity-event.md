This summer Prosperity Partners partnered with the Ronald McDonald House to work on their Care Kit Creation Program! Care kits are a great way for groups of all sizes and ages to get involved and support families. The families at Ronald McDonald House are often on the go between doctor appointments, hospital visits, and travel to and from the Houses. Care kits provide families with the essentials so they can stay by their child’s side. We ordered supplies to create 144 different kits, serving 144 different children and adults within the program! This was a great way for Prosperity Partners to make a difference in the lives of many families, all the while having fun with our coworkers!
