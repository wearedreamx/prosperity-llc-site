<p>Several of us here in the office are avid cyclists, and the rest of us just love a good time! Riding with Chicago Cycleboats means you earn your fun. Everyone is seated on a bicycle seat and you peddle as a group to propel the boat forward. Cycling as hard as we could, we could only power the boat to a whopping 5 mph!</p>
