We enjoyed the sustenance and suds of Lagunitas Brewing Company’s library and brewery tour. After relaxing into couches to taste the wide variety of brews the Chicago taproom had to offer, we took our glasses for a walk around the Pilsen production facility to learn more about the brewing process and the history of Lagunitas.
