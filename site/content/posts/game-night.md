With another successful spring tax season in the books, the NDH Team gathered at Hi-Point for some much needed fun, food, and drinks! We had our pick of a wide array of games from pinball, to skee-ball, to basketball, and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles! The night ended with our CEO Jeremy Dubow showing off his ping pong skills. It was so great to celebrate the end of the tax season and the arrival of spring!
