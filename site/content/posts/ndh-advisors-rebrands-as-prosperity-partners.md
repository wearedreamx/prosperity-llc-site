<p>CHICAGO —September 4, 2024 — NDH Advisors LLC (“NDH”), a Chicago-headquartered accounting firm, today announced that the company has rebranded as <a href="/">Prosperity Partners</a>, a name that aligns with the firm’s core values, commitment to top-tier client service and mission to be the leading destination for tax and accounting talent.</p>
<p>Founded in 2003 as “NDH” by three former Big Four tax professionals seeking to build a better accounting firm, the company quickly developed a reputation as a people-first organization that provides the highest caliber tax and accounting services to its sophisticated clientele. In 2023, following an equity investment from <a href="https://unitypartnerslp.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Unity Partners</a>, a Dallas-based private equity firm, NDH scaled its platform by investing heavily in infrastructure and technology and adding offices in Kansas City, Houston, Philadelphia, Santa Barbara, Detroit, and Washington D.C.</p>
<p>“The Prosperity Partners brand better captures the values, culture and ambitions of our organization, as shown in our recent accelerated growth —both ​organic and through strategic partnerships with other visionary leaders,” said Jeremy Dubow, CEO and co-founder of Prosperity Partners. “We’re proud to earn the trust of a growing number of partners and employees, while continuing to be the premier advisor to our outstanding clients.”</p>
<p>All employees of Prosperity Partners become part of Prosperity’s Employee Purpose Plan, a broad-based employee ownership program that allows employees to benefit from the company’s success.</p>
<p>“Our motto is ‘Together, let’s chase new horizons, create fresh opportunities, and shape a future where prosperity knows no bounds,” said Courtney Jones, head of Prosperity’s Kansas City office. “This statement resonates equally with our employees and clients, both of whom rely on us to be compassionate, innovative and accessible in meeting their needs and helping them prosper.”</p>
<p>“Since our investment in NDH eighteen months ago, we have prioritized implementing our ‘Partner &amp; Propel’ strategy to accelerate growth and to identify partners who embrace our goal of building better together as we expand the company’s footprint,” said Peter Cozzi, Team Lead at Unity Partners. “Now, with well over 100 employees in seven offices, we’re excited to go to market with a brand that speaks to Prosperity’s capabilities and growth.”</p>
