As part of our commitment to giving back, our Chicago team recently volunteered at San Miguel School Chicago, a school dedicated to helping underprivileged students succeed both academically and personally. Our team pitched in on several school projects — including decluttering spaces, painting, and beautifying common areas — all to help create a more welcoming environment for students and staff when they return from break. We’re proud to support organizations making a lasting impact and to be part of a firm that values community and service.
