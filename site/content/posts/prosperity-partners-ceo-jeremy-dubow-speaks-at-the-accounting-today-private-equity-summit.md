Last week, our very own CEO Jeremy Dubow had the privilege of speaking at the prestigious Accounting Today Private Equity Summit, the only conference dedicated to private equity investments in top accounting firms.

During his panel, Jeremy shared valuable insights on how to successfully navigate private equity transactions. He covered key topics such as identifying objectives when engaging with private equity, securing buy-in from partners and key stakeholders, evaluating and selecting the right private equity firms, and effectively communicating the benefits of private equity to employees and clients, among other critical strategies.

We are honored that Jeremy had the opportunity to represent Prosperity Partners at this influential summit, showcasing our commitment to fostering a successful private equity partnership, and demonstrating what a truly collaborative relationship can look like for accounting firm leaders!

More information about the PE Summit here: [https://conference.accountingtoday.com/event/pe-summit/summary](https://conference.accountingtoday.com/event/pe-summit/summary)
