<p>After wrapping up another successful tax season, our team came together in Chicago to celebrate the 4/15 deadline with an evening at Five Iron Golf. It was a great chance to connect outside of work, enjoy some friendly competition (virtual golf, soccer, hockey, dodgeball — you name it), and unwind over great food and drinks.</p>
<p>We’re proud of the hard work that went into this season and even more proud of the team behind it. Moments like these are a reminder of the culture we’ve built — one that values collaboration, connection, and celebrating success together.</p>
