<p>We NDHers wanted to give back to our community. And what better way to do that than to spend the day making gifts for rescue dogs and cats?<br>
We made fleece beds, dog pull-toys, and fun feathery toys for the cats to play with.<br>
After a tour of the facilities, during which we learned all about the Anti-Cruelty Society’s good work, we got to meet two of its residents! We were all enamored with Cookie the dog and Tom the cat.</p>
