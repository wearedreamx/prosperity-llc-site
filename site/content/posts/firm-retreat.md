To celebrate the firm’s 15-year Anniversary, the NDH team headed out to Harbor County in Southwest Michigan on the North Side for a weekend at the beautiful Goldberry Woods Bed & Breakfast. We explored the inn’s micro-farm, fruit and nut orchard, and hiking trails, and enjoyed a wine tasting and scavenger hunt! It was a beautiful trip that we’ll always remember!
