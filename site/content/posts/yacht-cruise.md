<p>For our first in-person event in over a year, we knew it had to be special. So we NDH-ers donned our sunscreen and boarded the Sophisticated Lady. It was a perfect day on Lake Michigan and a wonderful way to catch up. Captain Bill took us for a beautiful cruise all along the Chicago coast, from the South Shore to North Avenue Beach and back. Sun, drinks, and our NDH family – what more could you ask for?</p>
