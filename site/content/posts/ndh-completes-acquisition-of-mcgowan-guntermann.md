<p><strong>Acquisition of Tax &amp; Accounting Firm Bolsters Prosperity Partners’ Services and Establishes Presence in Santa Barbara</strong></p>
<p><strong>DALLAS—June 04, 2024</strong>—<a href="https://unitypartnerslp.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Unity Partners LP</a> (“Unity Partners”), a principles-based private equity firm, and Prosperity Partners Advisors LLC (“Prosperity Partners”), a Chicago-headquartered accounting business, today announced that Prosperity Partners has completed the acquisition of Santa Barbara-based tax and accounting firm McGowan Guntermann P.C. (“McGowan”). Terms of the private transaction were not disclosed.</p>
<p>Established in 1945 and now led by David Larson, McGowan provides tax and outsourced accounting services to high net-worth individuals, closely held businesses and their owners, and family groups. The current partners of McGowan will remain actively involved in the business going forward.</p>
<p>“We are thrilled to add the entire McGowan team to the Prosperity Partners family,” said Jeremy Dubow, CEO and co-founder of Prosperity Partners. “Managing Partner David Larson and his partners are talented and experienced leaders who run a high-quality business. Our client and service areas are very complementary and, more importantly, our firm cultures are well aligned.”</p>
<p>Consistent with Prosperity Partners’ people-first focus, all McGowan employees will become part of Prosperity Partners’ Employee Purpose Plan, a broad-based employee ownership program. As an essential aspect of each Unity Partners’ investment, the employee ownership plan allows qualifying employees to benefit from the company’s success.</p>
<p>“We were impressed by the Prosperity Partners team and the level of expertise and resources they could bring to bear,” said Larson. “Prosperity Partners is the right cultural and strategic fit for McGowan, and we believe our employees and clients will both benefit from being part of this team.”</p>
<p>“We’re confident that McGowan will be a fantastic addition to the Prosperity Partners platform,” said Peter Cozzi, Team Lead at Unity Partners. “It is exciting to see the Prosperity Partners team continue to attract such great partners, and we believe it is a testament to their ability to help already successful firms offer an even better experience to clients and employees.”</p>
