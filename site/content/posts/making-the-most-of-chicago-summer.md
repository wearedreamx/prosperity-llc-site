A few weeks ago, our Chicago team stepped away from the office for a day of sunshine, team bonding, and friendly competition at our Prosperity Summer Soiree at Castaways.

From relaxing on the top deck to enjoying volleyball, Spikeball, and ladder toss on the beach, it was the perfect opportunity to unwind and recharge together.

We couldn’t have asked for a better summer day or a better way to connect as a team before gearing up for another fall tax season!
