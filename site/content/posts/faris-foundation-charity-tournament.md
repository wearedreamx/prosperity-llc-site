<p>Our Sugar Land team dominated the courts in this fall to support children with cancer! Representatives Mike Jain (pickleball) and Gari Parikh (cornhole) both made it to the finals. While we’re proud of their athletic performance, we are even more proud of their support of a great cause. <a href="https://www.thefarisfoundation.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Faris Foundation</a> brightens children’s lives through childhood cancer research; creative arts programs at children’s cancer centers; public engagement and awareness around childhood cancer; and care packages for children with cancer. Well done, team!</p>
