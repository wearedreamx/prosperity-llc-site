<p>At Accounting Today’s Private Equity Summit, our CEO Jeremy Dubow shared how private equity (PE) has driven transformation within Prosperity Partners. Dubow discussed how Prosperity’s journey began with outreach and due diligence, ultimately leading to a partnership with Unity Partners LLC in 2023. He emphasized that private equity isn’t just about financial investment but about creating value for both the firm and its employees, allowing everyone to share in the firm’s growth.</p>
<p>Dubow also stressed the importance of exploring all options before committing to PE, advising firms not to jump in without careful consideration. He explained how private equity helped Prosperity think differently about technology and people, enabling our firm to accelerate change and become a “firm of the future.”</p>
