<p>In a recent episode of the Accounting ARC podcast, our CEO Jeremy Dubow shared insights on how private equity has been a driving force behind Prosperity Partners’ growth and innovative approach to accounting. Hosted by Donny Shimamoto, the podcast delved into the unique path Prosperity has taken to redefine the accounting profession.</p>
<p>Jeremy, who co-founded the firm in 2003 as NDH Advisors, explained how the company evolved after selling a stake to Unity Partners in 2023, rebranding as Prosperity Partners. He highlighted the firm’s focus on transformative technologies and a people-first culture as key factors in its success.</p>
<p>With private equity backing, Prosperity Partners has been able to streamline operations and focus on areas that drive significant value for clients. Through this innovative approach, the firm is redefining what an accounting firm can achieve in today’s ever-evolving market.</p>
