Our Chicago team gathered at Punch Bowl Social for our annual holiday party, enjoying an evening filled with great food, drinks, and a little friendly competition. From Mario Kart races to ping pong matches and even laser tag, the night was a fun way to connect in person and celebrate the relationships that make our culture so strong! We’re grateful for our amazing team and look forward to even more moments like this in 2026.
