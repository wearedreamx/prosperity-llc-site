<p>This May, team members from our Chicago and New York offices participated in the J.P. Morgan Corporate Challenge, enjoying an evening of fitness, camaraderie, and incredible skyline views.</p>
<p>We are proud of everyone who ran, volunteered, and cheered on their colleagues. Congratulations to our top finishers: Michael Payne and Shannon Richards from our New York office and Alyvia Clark from our Chicago office.</p>
<p>Thank you to all who participated and we look forward to being back at the starting line next year!</p>
