<p>Josh Harris is a co-founder of Prosperity Partners, where he leads the Client Accounting Services practice. He advises companies on various accounting matters, including financial analytics, reporting, and the development of Key Performance Indicators. Josh’s straightforward and high-value approach allows business owners to focus on their core operations while his team manages the accounting, monthly close, and financial analysis. With a background successfully running a professional services firm and an entrepreneurial spirit, Josh brings a fresh and innovative perspective to his work. His clients appreciate his accessibility and clear communication throughout their engagements. Besides client service, Josh spearheads the strategic recruitment initiative at Prosperity Partners and maintains responsibility for all aspects of the hiring process.</p>
<p>Before co-founding Prosperity Partners in 2003, Josh worked in the middle market tax groups at Arthur Andersen and Deloitte. Outside of work, Josh enjoys various water sports, spending quality time with his family, and volunteering in his community.</p>
