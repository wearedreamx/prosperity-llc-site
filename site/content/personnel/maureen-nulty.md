<p>Maureen graduated Suma Cum Laude from the University of Michigan Stephen M. Ross School of Business, with a secondary major in English. She began her career as a direct entry into the Tax Department of Ernst &amp; Young where she spent the next 13 years, ultimately achieving the role of Tax Principal. Maureen joined CCA (now Prosperity Partners) as a Tax Partner, continuing her focus for another 20+ years on highly technical real estate and other industry transactional activity.</p>
<p>Building on this rich and deep background in taxation and business transactions, Maureen now consults with the executive suite of family offices, often serving as interim CFO during periods of transition. Maureen’s CFO services provide a sounding board based on real-life experiences and knowledge to business owners and executives regarding strategic vision, wealth transfer, philanthropic initiatives, business transitions, succession planning, and entity structuring.</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE</strong>:</p>

<li>S-Corporation transactional planning, including deferred tax transactions and distribution and liquidation considerations</li>
</ul>
