Jennifer is a Certified Public Accountant specializing in tax compliance and consulting for high-net- worth individuals and small businesses. She holds a degree in business economics with an accounting emphasis from the University of California, Santa Barbara. Jennifer is a member of the California Society of Certified Public Accountants (CalCPA) and the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). She actively participates in CalCPA’s affinity program, enhancing her professional network and skills. Utilizing her extensive experience, Jennifer assists clients with diverse tax and accounting needs, offering strategic guidance tailored to their specific situations. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her family and friends, reading, and traveling.
