Nimesh earned his Bachelor’s Degree in Commerce from Mumbai University in 2018. He entered the field of Tax and Accounting in 2019 and has since gained three years of practical experience in Indian and U.S. Accounting, Taxation, Finance, and Audit. Before joining Prosperity, he served as a Process Associate at Thomson Reuters (part of SurePrep) where he was involved in U.S. Tax operations, focusing on the preparation and review of U.S. Individual Tax return forms 1040 and 1041. His professional experience reflects a strong commitment to accuracy, compliance, and timely delivery for a wide range of clients. He is keen to expand his expertise in U.S. Business Tax returns and U.S. Accounting.

**Expertise:** Indian Accounting and related software including Tally, Tally Prime, Winman, and WinTDS; U.S. Tax Compliance with Forms 1040 and 1041; Tax Software such as CCH, Go System, ProSystem, UltraTax, and SP Binder
