<p>Scout Precourt completed her Undergraduate and Master of Accountancy Degree at the University of Vermont. She interned at Danaher Attig &amp; Plante, PLC for three of her undergraduate years and ended up joining the firm as a fulltime employee upon graduation! Scout joined Prosperity Partners as a Senior Tax Associate in the spring of 2025, specializing in preparing and reviewing complex tax returns for high-net-worth individuals, trusts, estates, and businesses. However, she favors the challenge of estate and gift tax returns, which require a smart mix of accuracy, planning, and attention to what makes each case unique.</p>
<p>In her spare time, Scout enjoys spending time with her kids (Michael, Adalyn, Montie, and Imani). Taxes could never compete with the sound of their laughter. And when a rare quiet moment arises, she happily reclaims it for some much-needed rest!</p>
