Kavita Kharat completed her Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) from Mumbai University. During her studies, she developed a strong foundation in subjects like accounting, finance, and economics. She started her professional journey in US Tax at SurePrep working on individual and trust tax filings. This experience gave her a strong base in taxation and helped her better understand IRS guidelines. Her hard work earned her a promotion to Tax Reviewer, where she was responsible for reviewing tax returns, ensuring accuracy and compliance. In addition to her core responsibilities, she also led a team on the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) project. In 2018, Kavita received the opportunity to work in the US, which further enriched her professional experience and broadened her global exposure. After 23 years with SurePrep, she took the next step in her career by joining Prosperity’s VGS Team as an Assistant Tax Manager, where she continues to apply her knowledge and leadership skills in a dynamic environment.
