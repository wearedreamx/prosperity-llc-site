Leah joined McGowan Guntermann in 2021. She has worked in both private industry and public accounting, with a diverse client group of high-net-worth individuals, nonprofits, trusts, and S-corps. She assists clients with their cash flow management, general ledger management, financial statement preparation, and payroll and sales tax compliance. In addition, she works closely with CPAs to complete annual tax returns. Leah customizes financial reporting to fit her clients’ needs, which provides them with valuable insight into their businesses. She enjoys leveraging technology to improve efficiencies in the accounting processes for her clients. Leah received a bachelor of arts from University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB) and is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor. In her time away from the firm, she enjoys playing beach volleyball and traveling.

**EXPERTISE:** Personal Financial Accounting, Family Office Management, Small Business Accounting, Financial Statement Preparation
