As a Tax Manager, Owen works with a wide variety of clients, including Individuals, Estates, Trusts, Family Partnerships, and Small Businesses. A professional in the accounting field since 1985, Owen spent most of his career—28 years—with CPI Associates Inc. until they joined with Farkouh Furman & Faccio in 2018. He is looking forward to new challenges and opportunities with the recent merger of FF&F to Prosperity Partners.

An honest and dependable professional, Owen strives to focus on client relationships by putting his clients’ needs first. He is a valuable resource for his clients – they regularly seek him out for his opinion on business and personal financial matters. Owen chose to pursue a career in accounting because of the job stability that the field offers. He attributes his success to his attitude and ability to offer his clients what they want.

Owen lives in Hazlet, New Jersey with his wife of 36 years, Florence. They have three adult children.
