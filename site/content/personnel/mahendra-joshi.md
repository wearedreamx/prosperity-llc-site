<p>Mahendra Joshi is a seasoned tax professional with over 19 years of experience specializing in U.S. Individual and Fiduciary tax compliance. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce Degree from Mumbai University, where he built a strong foundation in Accounting, Finance, and Economics. Mahendra began his career at SurePrep, focusing on Individual and Trust tax filings. His dedication and performance quickly earned him a promotion to Tax Reviewer. He also played a key leadership role in the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) department, where he led a team, managed operations, and implemented process improvements. Since 2008, Mahendra has frequently traveled to the United States to support onshore operations, preparing and reviewing complex 1040 and 1041 tax returns. After nearly two decades at SurePrep, he brought his deep expertise and leadership to Prosperity’s VGS team as an Assistant Tax Manager, where he continues to contribute to high-quality tax compliance and team development.</p>
