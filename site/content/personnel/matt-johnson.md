After 4 years in the United States Coast Guard, Matt graduated from Lyndon State College with a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting. After graduation, he moved to Washington DC where he worked his way to Partner in a CPA firm. Following 12 years in Washington DC, he decided to move back to Vermont, where he currently serves as a Partner at Prosperity. Matt provides tax and estate planning services for high-net-worth individuals, real estate businesses, and real estate investors. During his spare time, Matt can be found spending time with his family or on the golf course.
