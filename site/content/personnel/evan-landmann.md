Evan graduated from Loyola University Chicago in 2016 with a Bachelor of Business Administration in Accounting and a Minor in Spanish. Before transitioning into public accounting in 2018, he spent two years as a tax compliance and debt resolution specialist. He specializes in tailored tax advisory and financial consulting for individuals, families, businesses, and trusts, with a particular focus on high-net-worth families. Evan joined the Prosperity team as a Manager in July 2025 and holds licensure as both a Certified Public Accountant through the AICPA and an Enrolled Agent with the IRS.

**EXPERTISE:** Pass-Through, Individual, Fiduciary & Estate Taxation; Gift Tax Compliance & Planning; General Tax Consulting & Planning; Private Foundations

- **Favorite thing about my job:** I enjoy the human side of tax and tax planning. Behind every set of returns is an individual—or a family—with unique goals and circumstances. Designing customized strategies that help clients reach those goals is both an engaging challenge and incredibly rewarding.
