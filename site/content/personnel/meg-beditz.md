<p>Meg joined Farkouh, Furman &amp; Faccio as an Administrative Assistant in 2014, after which her role evolved to a primary focus in operations and business development. Her early involvement in nearly all facets of practice management—including, but not limited to, client relations, billing, recruiting, and marketing—has contributed to her deep understanding of operations for professional services and the requirements needed to provide top tier client service. She was promoted to COO in 2025 before joining Prosperity Partners as VP of Operations in 2026.</p>
<p>She obtained a B.S. in English with minors in Communications and Journalism from State University of New York (SUNY) College at Oneonta, where she played softball for four years and served as senior captain. She also holds an M.S. in Publishing from Pace University.</p>
<p>Meg’s favorite sport is baseball, and she’s a lifelong New York Yankees fan (with high standards set by their 1996 and 1998 dream teams). Don’t mention the World Baseball Classic around her unless you want a dissertation. When she’s not playing softball or trying a new food spot, Meg most enjoys traveling, attending concerts, painting, baking, exploring nature, and, above all, maximizing time with the friends and family who fill her cup.</p>
