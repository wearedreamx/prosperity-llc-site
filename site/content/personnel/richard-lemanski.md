Richard Lemanski is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) with extensive experience in both auditing and tax preparation. He graduated from George Mason University with a degree in Accounting and quickly passed the CPA exam, aided by a review course led by his professors. Richard began his career working for various local, regional, and national CPA firms, where he gained valuable experience as an auditor and tax preparer.

He later joined Wiener and Garg CPAs, where he focused on his passion for tax work. Over the years, Richard has developed a deep expertise in the tax treatment of real estate development and rental activities and has enjoyed working with a diverse range of businesses and individuals throughout the Washington, DC area.

Outside of his professional life, Richard enjoys hiking with his dogs along the Potomac River trails in Northern Virginia and spending time at home working on small projects.

**EXPERTISE:** Real Estate Tax, Rental Income, Business and Individual Tax
