Ella Midenjak graduated from University of Vermont in 2010 with a Bachelors in Business Administration. Post graduation, Ella went into retail management, becoming a Store Manager of a supermarket store before transitioning to Human Resources. After nearly 6 years in HR, she joined Danaher Attig & Plante in 2023, now Prosperity Partners. As a member of the Burlington Prosperity team, Ella loves helping clients and especially celebrating office birthdays!
