Maria graduated from Andres Bello Catholic University in Venezuela with a bachelor’s degree in public accounting. In Venezuela, Maria worked as an auditor in a public accounting firm and held roles in financial reporting and accounts receivables with several international companies in the finance sector. Maria moved to the United States in 2018. She joined Prosperity in 2023 as an Administrative Assistant in their Houston office. In this role, she supports the accounting and tax teams with various tasks including client outreach, communicating with compliance agencies, and ensuring the timely processing and assembly of accounting and tax projects. In her spare time, Maria enjoys spending time with friends, walking, and enjoying nature.

**EXPERTISE** – Administration, Customer Service, Account Receivables.
