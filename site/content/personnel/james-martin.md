James Martin graduated from Eastern Michigan University with a BA in Accounting and an MS in Accounting Information Systems. He worked for Chrysler Financial Corporation prior to joining Deloitte as a Senior Manager. In 2002, he began at Cendrowski Corporate Advisors, and now serves as a Senior Manager with Prosperity Partners.

**EXPERTISE**: Financial Accounting, Internal controls and business processes, Forensic Accounting, Litigation support

- **Favorite thing about my job**: What I enjoy most about my job is the variety of cases and matters with which I am involved. Every day is different in some way.
- **What is the best dish you can cook?** The best dish I can cook is New England Clam Chowder. I learned the recipe from the restaurant I worked at in high school.
- **Favorite vacation you’ve taken**: When I was in high school, I traveled with our Spanish class to the Yucatan Peninsula. This was before Cancun turned into the destination resort it is today, and was still a small, quiet beach town. We toured the Mayan sites, and it was beautiful.
- **What is your side gig/passion project/hobby?** Since 2001 I have coached the ACHA Men’s Division 2 Hockey team at Michigan State University! We compete against other collegiate teams across the United States.
