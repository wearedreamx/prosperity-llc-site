<p>Sydney Hallums graduated from the University of South Florida in 2021 with a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting. She began her career with a tax-focused internship at a regional firm in Middle Tennessee where she was raised. Sydney always anticipated living in Florida, and she got the opportunity to take a full-time Tax Associate position in Melbourne, Florida in 2021. She was pleased to join Danaher, Attig, &amp; Plante, PLC as a remote Tax Preparer in 2023, eventually becoming part of the Prosperity Partners team in 2025. She currently serves as a Tax Associate and is working towards her CPA certification.</p>
<p>In her free time, Sydney enjoys spending time with her friends and family either by the beach, pool, or at an amusement park. She is very grateful that working remotely allows her flexibility to spend time with her favorite guy – her corgi named Colin!</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE:</strong> Individual, Trust, and Gift Tax Preparation</p>
