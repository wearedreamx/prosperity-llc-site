Michael Clark is a Certified Public Accountant with a Master’s in Professional Accounting from Colorado State University Global and a Bachelor’s degree from the University of Alabama. Since 2015, he has specialized in tax planning, preparing individual and business returns, and providing tax projections to help clients minimize their tax liability. Michael joined Prosperity Partners in October 2024, where he is dedicated to offering personalized advice and strategies to ensure clients make the most of their financial opportunities. Outside of the office, Michael enjoys perfecting his golf game and cheering on Alabama football—Roll Tide!
