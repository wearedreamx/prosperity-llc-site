<p>Before joining Prosperity Partners, Cathy co-founded Danaher Attig &amp; Plante PLC in 2005, following a decade at KPMG LLP. Her practice centers on Income, Gift, and Estate Planning and Compliance, with a specialized focus on the unique needs of affluent families and business owners.</p>
<p>She holds a B.S. in Accounting from Salisbury University in Salisbury, Maryland. Cathy is a member of both the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) and the Vermont Society of CPAs.</p>
<p>Outside of work, Cathy enjoys boating on Lake Champlain, reading, and spending time with her family in Vermont.</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE:</strong> Tax Planning and Compliance for Closely Held Businesses, High Net Worth Individuals, and Family Groups including Trusts, Estates, and Private Foundations</p>
