Before joining Prosperity Partners, Cathy co-founded Danaher Attig & Plante PLC in 2005, following a decade at KPMG LLP. Her practice centers on Income, Gift, and Estate Planning and Compliance, with a specialized focus on the unique needs of affluent families and business owners.

She holds a B.S. in Accounting from Salisbury University in Salisbury, Maryland. Cathy is a member of both the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) and the Vermont Society of CPAs.

Outside of work, Cathy enjoys boating on Lake Champlain, reading, and spending time with her family in Vermont.

**EXPERTISE:** Tax Planning and Compliance for Closely Held Businesses, High Net Worth Individuals, and Family Groups including Trusts, Estates, and Private Foundations
