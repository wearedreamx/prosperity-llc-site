Rohnish Santosh Shinde is a Verification Associate at Prosperity with a Master’s Degree in Advanced Accounting and a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting and Finance from Vidyalankar Institute of Information Technology. He began his professional journey in 2021, specializing in U.S. individual tax verification and preparation using SurePrep, a Thomson Reuters platform. In November 2024, Rohnish joined Instalogic Solution Private Limited as an Audit Consultant, contributing to high-impact government projects for the Government of Maharashtra. His work focused on Financial Impact Assessments, where he analyzed budget allocations, resource utilization, and financial outcomes to provide data-driven policy recommendations for improving governance. He also led social audits of welfare schemes, evaluating program effectiveness, transparency, and alignment with intended goals. His insights played a key role in workplace implementations and enhancing accountability within public service delivery. In August 2025, Rohnish took on the role of Verification Associate, bringing his analytical expertise and commitment to precision into the verification domain.

**Expertise:** U.S. Individual Tax Verification & Preparation, Financial Impact Assessments, Social Audit and Policy Evaluation
