Rangoli Sen graduated with a Bachelor of Science Degree and is a Chartered Accountant from India. She started her career at PwC Consulting and worked as a finance professional at Watson Wyatt (now Towers Watson) in Gurgaon, Kolkata India. After moving to the US, she passed the US CPA exams and has worked for 10+ years as a tax professional/supervisor for boutique public accounting firms in NJ.

She joined Prosperity Partners in December 2025 as part of the merger between Prosperity Partners and Mizrach Group (Dorfman Mizrach and Thaler), taking on the role of Senior Tax Associate.
