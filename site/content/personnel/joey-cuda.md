Joey Cuda graduated from the University of Baylor with a B.S in Biology, later receiving his Certificate in Accounting from the University of Virginia. He started his accounting career in assurance services at BDO, serving Private Equity and Government Contracting clients before joining Pipaya in 2017. At Pipaya (now Prosperity) he leads Sell-Side, Buy-Side, and M&A consulting projects. He is a licensed CPA in the state of Virginia. Outside of work, Joey enjoys traveling to national parks, watching baseball and college football, and visiting theme parks with his wife.

**EXPERTISE:** Mergers and Acquisitions, Government Contracting
