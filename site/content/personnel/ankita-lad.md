Ankita received her Bachelor’s degree in Commerce from Mumbai University in 2020. She began her tax career in 2021 and has gained over three years of hands-on experience in U.S. tax preparation. Prior to joining VGSL (part of Prosperity), she worked full-time as a Process Associate at Thomson Reuters, where she was working for their client CBIZ. Previously, she also held a role as a Tax Associate at Datamatics Global Services Ltd. Throughout her career, she’s been involved in preparing and directly submitting individual (Form 1040) and business tax returns (Forms 1065, 1120S, and 1120C) for clients.
