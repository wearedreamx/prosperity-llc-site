<p>Andy joined Farkouh, Furman &amp; Faccio in 1998 as a Staff Accountant and has spent his entire professional career with the firm, including the recent transition to Prosperity Partners. Andy currently serves as a Partner, and though he primarily services individuals, closely held businesses, and corporations, he has experience with tax compliance for nearly every entity type.</p>
<p>Initially deciding to pursue accounting due to his aptitude for numbers, Andy most enjoys his field because it offers him an opportunity to help his clients identify meaningful solutions for their tax and financial challenges. Accrediting his success to dedication and caring tremendously about each of his clients, his motivation stems from a desire to not only provide his clients with timely knowledge and expertise, but to create lasting relationships with each of them.</p>
<p>Raised in Queens, Andy currently lives in Flushing, NY, with his wife and two sons. His favorite thing about living in New York City is its fast pace and abundance of good food. In addition to spending time with his family, Andy enjoys watching sports, anime, and reading Sci-Fi in his spare time.</p>
