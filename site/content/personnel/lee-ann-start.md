Lee Ann Start has over 20 years of experience in the tax and accounting field at both large and small firms, as well as working as a Business Manager for a non-profit organization for several years. She graduated Magna Cum Laude from St. Michael’s College in 2003 with a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting and Minor in English Literature. She passed the CPA exam that same year and became a licensed CPA in 2009 after meeting the remaining licensure requirements. Lee Ann is a member of the American Institute of CPAs and the Vermont Society of CPAs. In her free time, she enjoys gardening and spending time with her family!

**EXPERTISE:** Fiduciary, Gift, and Individual Tax Compliance
