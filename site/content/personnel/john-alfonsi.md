John Alfonsi graduated from the University of Michigan-Dearborn with a concentration in Accounting. He also has a Master’s of Science in Taxation from Walsh College, where he was the Research Institute of America Graduate Tax Award recipient. He brings over 40 years of experience in tax and advisory services, focusing his practice on business valuation, economic damages, and tax planning and consulting for pass-through entities. John is a recognized financial expert in federal and various state circuit courts across the country. In his spare time, he is an adjunct professor at his Alma Mater, teaching master’s degree courses in taxation and valuation.

**EXPERTISE**

- Business valuation for business and marital divorce purposes, estate/gift/income tax planning and compliance, buy-sell agreements, business mergers and acquisitions
- Economic damages calculations including lost profits and lost value in matters involving breach of contract, IP infringement, and other contractual claims
- Tax planning and compliance for investment entities including venture capital funds, hedge funds, real estate ownership and operation, and private equity funds
- Succession planning consulting
