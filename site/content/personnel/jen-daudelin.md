<p>Jen Daudelin is a Tax Associate with over four years of experience primarily preparing personal and partnership tax returns. She began her career in bookkeeping for small businesses, then gained public service experience as an Assistant Town Clerk. Jen holds an Associate’s Degree in Accounting from Champlain College. In her free time, she enjoys reading, baking, making strawberry jam (just like her Grandma used to make!), walking her dog, and spending time with her family.</p>
