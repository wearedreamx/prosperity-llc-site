Julia is an accomplished tax manager specializing in trust and estates, tax compliance, and business planning. She graduated from UCSB with a bachelor’s degree in Global Studies and in 2015 earned her master’s in business taxation from USC. Beyond the office, she serves as Managing Director at the Santa Barbara Beekeepers Association and serves on the local and state-wide committees at the California Society of CPA’s (CalCPA). Julia is passionate about mentoring young professionals and promoting sustainability through beekeeping.
