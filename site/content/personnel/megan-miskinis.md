<p>Megan graduated Cum Laude from the University of South Carolina in 2012, with her bachelor’s of science in business administration with a focus in accounting. The following year, Megan graduated from the University of Maryland with a master’s of science in business and management: accounting. Megan joined Prosperity Partners in August of 2023 as an Accounting Manager, specializing in providing outsourced accounting services for a variety of clients including start-ups, small businesses, and personal accounting for high-net-worth individuals. Prior to Prosperity Partners, Megan worked in the Assurance practice at EY in Washington DC, where she spent 3 years working on a variety of public clients. She met her husband and relocated to Pennsylvania where she started a job with Baker Tilly in the Assurance practice working on primarily private clients in the hospitality, franchise, and financial industries. After almost six years, Megan ventured into the client accounting services industry working for Williams Marston, based out of Boston, MA for one year before relocating to South Bend, IN for her husband’s job and starting at Prosperity Partners. In her spare time, Megan likes to go for bike rides with her family, go to Lake Michigan, and golf.</p>
