Alyvia Clark joined Prosperity Partners in the summer of 2014 as an intern and in her tenure with the firm has gained valuable experience in a wide variety of financial accounting and tax related areas. As she has matured in her career, Alyvia now leads the Family Office Services practice at Prosperity Partners and focuses primarily on high net worth individuals and accounting matters within the firm. Alyvia prides herself on being a well-rounded advisor who earns the trust of her clients by building meaningful relationships and being a reliable, informed consultant.

Alyvia graduated from Loyola University Chicago with a bachelor’s degree in accounting and a minor in marketing. She continued her academic career at Loyola earning her Master’s in Business Administration in 2016. In addition to her education, Alyvia acted as a graduate assistant coach for the cross country and track & field teams at Loyola, where she competed as an undergraduate.

In her free time, Alyvia enjoys running through the neighborhoods of Chicago, tackling jigsaw puzzles, hiking in the American west, and traveling to new places.
