Mary Beth attended Champlain College, majoring in the Executive Assistant program. She started her career in the banking industry as an Administrative Assistant on both the bank and corporate sides. While at the bank, she was able to transition into the legal department as a Legal Assistant, which then lead her to return to Champlain College and obtain her Paralegal Certification! She joined Danaher Attig & Plante in 2014 and now serves as a Tax Administrator for the Prosperity Partners team. Mary Beth always looks forward to the tax season and working with our clients to make it as smooth as possible.

**EXPERTISE:** Administration
