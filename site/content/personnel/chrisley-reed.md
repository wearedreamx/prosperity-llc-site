<p>Chris Reed graduated University of California, Santa Barbara with a bachelor’s degree in Business Economics in 1987. He joined McGowan Guntermann in 1989 after receiving his CPA license and was promoted to a partner of the McGowan Guntermann – Audit and Accounting group in 1999. Chris has expertise in accounting, auditing, and taxation for not-for-profit organizations and employee benefit plans. Chris is a past chair of the California State Peer Review Committee where he served 18 years as a committee member and performed firm-on-firm peer reviews for over 20 years. Chris and his wife, Barbara, love traveling, by air, land, and sea.</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE:</strong> Not-for-profit accounting and taxation for public charities and private foundations.</p>
