Michelle Cantrell started her Bachelor’s in Business Administration with a focus in Finance at DeVry University and finished online with Rasmussen University while working a full-time job in the Mortgage Industry. Additionally, she completed a Mid-Level Leadership Program from Rockhurst University sponsored by The Labor Management Council of Greater Kansas City. The past 6 years she has worked in the Industrial Construction Industry as a Midwest Regional Accounting Manager. Michelle is excited to join Prosperity Partners and for the change in career to have better work-life balance. In her spare time, she enjoys hiking, kayaking, pretty much anything outdoors, and taking in live music at one of the local bars here in Kansas City!
