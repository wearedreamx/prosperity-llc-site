John Rovegno graduated from St. John’s University with a Bachelors and Master’s Degree in Accounting. He brings over 15 years of experience in Tax and Advisory services, focusing on Income Tax provisions, Domestic and Foreign Corporate Tax compliance, and Partnerships. In his spare time, he enjoys chasing around his 3 young daughters to their respective sporting events and dance recitals.

EXPERTISE: Income Tax Provisions, Domestic and Foreign Corporate Reporting and Tax Compliance, Partnership Taxation, Corporate State and Local Tax

**Favorite thing about my job:** Getting a front row seat to how successful businesses are run. There’s also a quiet thrill in knowing you’ve saved a company real money while everyone else just sees “magic numbers.” Plus, nothing builds character like explaining why the tax answer is “it depends” and meaning it with your whole soul.
