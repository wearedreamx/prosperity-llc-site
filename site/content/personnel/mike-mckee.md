Mike McKee is a Tax Director at Prosperity Partners with more than 20 years of public accounting experience. He works primarily with high-net-worth individuals, partnerships, and privately held businesses on tax planning and compliance matters. Mike enjoys working through complicated tax issues and making them easier for his clients to understand. He graduated from Goldey-Beacom College in Wilmington, Delaware, and is a Certified Public Accountant licensed in Pennsylvania. Outside of work, Mike enjoys running, traveling, and kayaking/paddleboarding with his son.

**EXPERTISE**: Tax Planning and Compliance, High-Net-Worth Individuals, Partnership Taxation, Multi-state Taxation

**Favorite Thing About My Job**: I enjoy solving problems. When a tax issue gets complicated, I like digging into it, figuring out the best approach, and explaining it in a way the client can understand. I also enjoy the relationships I’ve built with clients and coworkers over the years!

**Favorite Vacation you’ve taken**: Traveling to Ireland with my family. It was quite an adventure, to put it mildly, and we came home with some great stories.

**What is your side gig/passion project/hobby?** I enjoy running and being outside. Kayaking and paddleboarding with my son are two of my favorite ways to spend a nice day.

**What would the title of your autobiography be?** “*Amended and Dangerous.*” I’m a tax guy who grew up watching 1980s action movies, so it seems appropriate!

**If you were a board game, what game would you be?** Monopoly. I’m competitive, I like strategy, and I would probably spend too much time thinking about the tax consequences of every deal.

**Favorite Workout or Sport to Play**: Running! I like having a race on the calendar and something specific to train toward.
