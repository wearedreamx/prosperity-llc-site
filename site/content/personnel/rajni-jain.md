<p>Rajni graduated with a master’s degree in accounting, from Punjab University in India. She began her career as a Professor of Financial Accounting and Business Management and subsequently served as the Dean of the Business School at Khalsa College for Men, Ludhiana, India. Rajni came to the US after getting married to Mike Jain in 1983. &nbsp;After moving to the US, she started working in public accounting and earned her CPA. In January 1986, Rajni and Mike started their own practice – Jain &amp; Jain, P.C. Rajni supports the CAS team in Houston and has led the charge in identifying and implanting various outsourcing solutions for accounting and bookkeeping services. Under her leadership, the outsourced function continues to grow in the breadth and depth of services they are able to provide.</p>
<p>In her spare time, Rajni loves to sing Bollywood songs and leads several Karaoke groups. She enjoys spending time with close family, friends, and clients and loves to foster and build new relationships. She has a knack for bringing people together and never shies away from an opportunity to host an event at her home – whether staff holiday celebrations, social dinners with clients, fund-raising events for various political officials, or equity-raising for new business ventures.</p>
