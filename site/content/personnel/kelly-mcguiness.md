Kelly McGuiness is an accounting and systems professional specializing in controllership, automation, and scalable finance operations for high-growth companies. She holds both a BS with a double concentration in Accounting and Information Systems, and an MS in Accounting from Boston College. Kelly has led systems integrations, intercompany accounting initiatives, and audit readiness projects that improved accuracy, transparency, and efficiency across global entities. Outside the office, Kelly bakes sourdough and runs a book & crafts club with her friends in Miami!
