Liz Butler serves as Prosperity’s Director of Operations and leads the firm’s Learning and Development initiatives. She’s passionate about lifelong learning and fostering a culture of learning and development at Prosperity. Prior to the acquisition, Liz spent 12 years as the Director of Operations for Pipaya, building out the entire back-office operations of the firm. Liz is a CPA and began her career as an Auditor with Arthur Andersen. Other career highlights include working in Accounting Operations at various companies in the commercial finance, hospitality, real estate, and healthcare industries. She graduated from the University of Virginia McIntire School of Commerce with a Bachelors Degree in Commerce and a concentration in Accounting. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with family, crafting, and decorating beautiful spaces at home.

**EXPERTISE**: Talent Development, Operations, Strategic Execution
