Jeremy Dubow is Prosperity Partners’ Chief Executive Officer. He manages the company and is responsible for its strategic direction, growth initiatives and talent development. He began his career at Arthur Andersen before co-founding Prosperity in 2003.

As a tax expert, he provides sophisticated tax planning and consulting services to technology entrepreneurs and their enterprises, high net worth individuals and family offices. Jeremy also co-leads Prosperity’s [mergers and acquisitions practice](/tax-services/transaction-advisory-services/), advising business owners throughout the sales process.

Jeremy routinely speaks on behalf of the IL CPA Society and AICPA on a variety of tax technical topics and is actively involved in the ILCPAS tax committees. During his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his family, cycling and playing pickleball; he is also Prosperity’s premier table tennis player.

**EXPERTISE:** Mergers & Acquisitions, Ultra-high Net Worth Tax Planning, Equity Compensation, Partnership Taxation, Estate Planning, State & Local Taxation
