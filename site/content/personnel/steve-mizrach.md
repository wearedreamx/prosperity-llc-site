<p>At twenty-three years of age, Steven Mizrach was one of the youngest people in New York State to be made Partner. Steve was previously the Partner-In-Charge of the Iselin office of a regional accounting and consulting firm, before securing Partner at Dorfman Mizrach &amp; Thaler, LLP (DMT). In December 2025, the Mizrach team joined forces with Prosperity Partners, where he has remained a Partner and leader of the Iselin team.</p>
<p>Steve has extensive experience in dealing with the business community in matters that include structuring transactions, financing, financial analysis, and “visionary thinking.” Steve has a broad background in Taxation and Accounting for companies in the technology, manufacturing, transportation, publishing, advertising, real estate, healthcare, and entertainment industries. A perfectionist and excellent negotiator, Steve’s forte lies in his unparalleled ability to deal with complex transactions that develop into life-altering events for the clients.</p>
<p>Steve is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, the New Jersey State Society of Certified Public Accountants, and the New Jersey Medical Group Management Association. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Brooklyn College as well as a Master’s degree in Taxation from Pace University. Steve is a licensed CPA in both New Jersey and New York. He has lectured extensively for many professional and trade organizations, and frequently appears on television and radio as a Financial Commentator.</p>
