Bhadresh Shukal is a graduate of Mumbai University, where he specialized in Accounting and Taxation. He began his professional journey at an outsourcing firm focused on U.S. taxation, steadily building expertise in accounting and business tax returns. His career path led him to Thomson Reuters, where he held the role of Team Leader and Reviewer. In February 2025, Bhadresh joined Vignesh Global Services LLC, where he now focuses on preparing and reviewing both business and individual tax returns. With a strong command over partnership, corporation, and individual tax compliance, Bhadresh is committed to deepening his knowledge and enjoys working directly with Prosperity Partners to enhance his professional capabilities. Outside of work, Bhadresh cherishes vacation time with family and friends. One of his most memorable trips was a 14-day journey through North India, including visits to Kashmir, the Golden Temple, and Wagah Border.
