Doug graduated from Duke University with a degree in economics. He began his career as a management consultant, first with PwC and then with Treacy & Company (now part of Cherry Bekaert). Doug then transitioned into private equity, joining Audax Group and then Investindustrial. Since then, Doug has focused on acquisitions of small and mid-sized businesses in people-driven industries including insurance brokerage, healthcare services and now tax and accounting services at Prosperity Partners where Doug leads our inorganic growth efforts and strategic partnerships.

**EXPERTISE:** Mergers & Acquisitions

- **Favorite thing about my job:** Listening to the stories of business owners and leaders. The entrepreneurial journey is always unique and learning how businesses have grown, evolved and overcome challenges along the way is fascinating.
- **What is the best dish you can cook:** I make steak au poivre on occasion. More often than not that occasion is when I’m home alone for the evening, so feedback on the meal is rare.
- **Favorite vacation you’ve taken:** My delayed honeymoon to Australia and New Zealand. It’s a long way to go but worth it if you have the time.
- **What would your last meal be:** A Thanksgiving style meal – turkey, cranberry sauce, stuffing, mashed potatoes, etc. Perhaps it’s nostalgia but I look forward to it every year.
- **Favorite workout or sport to play:** I love to ski. I try to get to Colorado twice a year, once in the winter and once in the spring. Life can get in the way of those plans but I’m always optimistic about next year.
