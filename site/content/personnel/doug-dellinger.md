<p>Doug graduated from Duke University with a degree in economics. He began his career as a management consultant, first with PwC and then with Treacy &amp; Company (now part of Cherry Bekaert). Doug then transitioned into private equity, joining Audax Group and then Investindustrial. Since then, Doug has focused on acquisitions of small and mid-sized businesses in people-driven industries including insurance brokerage, healthcare services and now tax and accounting services at Prosperity Partners where Doug leads our inorganic growth efforts and strategic partnerships.</p>
