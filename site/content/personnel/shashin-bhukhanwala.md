Shashin Bhukhanwala graduated from Mumbai University with a degree in Accounting and Economics. After graduating, he pursued his Chartered Accountancy from the ICAI and cleared his Intermediate examinations. He joined SurePrep India (Pvt) ltd in 2004, heading a project for one of the biggest clients of the company for 7 years. For many years, he was a part of the testing team that tested 1040 SCAN products before being released for use. After working for more than 20 years with SurePrep, he joined Prosperity’s VGS team as a Tax Manager.
