<p>Karen Maddaloni graduated Cum Laude from Bernard Baruch College in 2007, earning a Bachelor of Arts in Spanish with a Minor in Psychology. Karen brings over 20 years of experience in the administrative field, joining the New York team in December of 2011 as an Administrative Assistant before being promoted to the role of Firm Administrator in 2015. In 2023, she was promoted to the role of Director of Staff Administration, where she oversees the New York Administrative team with a concentration in resource management. Karen has over 10 years’ experience in staffing and scheduling client engagements based on complexity and staff expertise. In her free time, her favorite thing to do is to travel to new places and take road trips with her family.</p>
