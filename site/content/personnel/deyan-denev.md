<p>Deyan joined Prosperity and began practicing tax as an associate in 2014. He holds a bachelor’s degree in accounting and a master’s in taxation from DePaul University. Currently a senior tax manager, Deyan is a tax generalist with a robust knowledge base who specializes in tax compliance and consulting. He provides his clients with high-level comprehensive advice, guides them through complex tax issues and presents them with efficient tax-planning opportunities and strategies.</p>
<p>His business clients span across a variety of different industries from IT and general business-consulting companies to electronics manufacturers, to large family investment partnerships. Deyan closely works his with high-net worth and ultra-high-net worth individual clients, guiding them in making smart tax decisions and ensuring they’re employing robust tax-saving strategies that minimize their tax burden, and help grow their wealth. Additionally, as member of the Illinois CPA Society’s International Committee, Deyan has a special interest in international taxation with notable experience in taxation of U.S. expatriates and foreign nationals.</p>
<p>When not in the office, Deyan enjoys spending time with his family, traveling as much as time allows and reading a good fiction book.</p>
