After graduating from college, he joined Pipaya (now part of Prosperity Partners) to pursue his interest in mergers & acquisitions and transaction advisory. He is passionate about helping clients navigate a successful transaction and enjoys the learning and problem solving unique to each deal. In his spare time, he enjoys traveling, hiking, and cheering for the Eagles, Phillies and 76ers!
