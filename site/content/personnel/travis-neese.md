Travis Neese graduated from Millikin University with a double major in Theatre and Human Services, with minors in Psychology and Sociology. Shortly after graduating, Travis moved to the Windy City to continue his journey as a theatre artist while also working in Early Childhood Education. He went on to pursue his master’s degree in Guidance and Counseling from The University of West Alabama. He made the transition out of education in the summer of 2023, beginning his journey at Prosperity Partners as an Office Manager. He is still heavily active in the theatre and film communities in Chicago not only as an actor, but as a writer and director as well. In his spare time, he enjoys walking his dogs with his partner, exploring new restaurants, and attending as much theatre as possible.
