<p>Coby Haim graduated from the University of New York – College of Staten Island with a Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting. Shortly after graduating, he obtained his CPA and began his career in a small accounting firm where he serviced the average “mom &amp; pop” taxpayer. Eager to expand his knowledge, he later moved to a mid-size firm in New York City where he gained experience in the taxation of ultra-high net worth and high net worth individuals, privately held businesses, and various investment funds and the planning that comes along with it. After a 5-year tenure, Coby decided his passion was to help mid-size businesses build wealth and create the “next generation” of high-net-worth taxpayers. In 2019, he joined Dorfman, Mizrach, &amp; Thaler, LLP, now merged with Prosperity Partners. Here he has gained expertise in various closely-held operating industries and provides planning, consulting, and tax services to both the businesses and their owners.</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE</strong>: Pass-through Taxation, Mergers &amp; Acquisitions, Tax and Estate Planning, High-Net-Worth Individuals, Tax Resolution and Representation before Taxing Authorities</p>
