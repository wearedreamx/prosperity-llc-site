Cheryl Chambers earned her degree in Business Administration from Champlain College in Burlington, Vermont. She began her career in finance and banking before stepping away to raise her family. Cheryl later reentered the workforce as an intern at KPMG LLP, where she spent several years honing her tax skills before joining Danaher Attig & Plante PLC in 2005. Over the years, she advanced from Tax Preparer to Senior Tax Associate, and ultimately to Tax Manager.

Today, Cheryl is a Tax Manager at Prosperity Partners, where she is a highly valued member of the team! She brings extensive experience and a deep understanding of Fiduciary, Estate, Gift, and High-Net-Worth Individual tax returns.

**EXPERTISE:** Fiduciary, Estate, and Gift Tax; High-Net-Worth Individual Returns
