Neha Brahmania is an Enrolled Agent (EA) certified by the IRS with expertise in Taxation and Tax Compliance. She also holds a Master’s Degree of Commerce from Mumbai University. Upon graduation, she joined SurePrep India Pvt. Ltd. where she laid the foundation for her journey in US Taxation, working primarily with Individual and Fiduciary Tax filings. She spent a significant part of her career at Sure Prep as a Tax Reviewer, gaining experience in reviewing returns for accuracy, consistency, and adherence to IRS rules and deadlines. She had the opportunity to work as part of the Tax Technology team, where she was involved in testing the company’s proprietary application, SP Binder, contributing to multiple updates to enhance the application. Since 2009, Neha has frequently traveled to the United States (mostly California and Arizona) for onshore busy season operations, preparing and reviewing complex 1040 and 1041 tax returns. Neha has recently joined Prosperity’s VGSL team as a Tax Reviewer, where she continues to contribute to high-quality tax compliance and team development.
