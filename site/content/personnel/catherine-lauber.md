<p>Catherine has been a dedicated professional in the accounting industry since graduating with a bachelor’s in economics and accounting from the University of California, Santa Barbara in 2012. Initially serving local businesses and individuals in the Santa Barbara, California area, she expanded her practice to include Missoula, Montana in 2021. With a strong background in bookkeeping prior to becoming a CPA, she brings a wealth of hands-on experience advising business owners on accounting matters.</p>
<p>Her passion for tax and financial analysis drives her to provide exceptional service to her clients, ensuring they make informed financial and tax-effective decisions year-round. Catherine works closely with investment advisors, business managers, and individuals, bringing teams together to make efficient and strategic tax planning decisions.</p>
<p>Catherine specializes in advising clients on the Real Estate Professional status, 1031 exchange tax deferral, and accelerated depreciation strategies. Her expertise in these areas enables clients to optimize tax benefits, differentiate between passive and nonpassive losses, and with designing tailored tax optimization plans.</p>
<p>Beyond her professional achievements, Catherine is an active community member, serving on the boards of three local nonprofits. When she’s not in the office, you can find Catherine rafting on the Clark Fork River, mountain biking trails across the United States, or throwing pottery on her ceramics wheel.</p>
<p><strong>EXPERTISE:</strong> Flow-through Taxation, 1031 Exchange Transactions and Real Estate Professional Elections, Individual and Business Tax Compliance and Planning, and Strategic Tax Planning for Stock Options.</p>
