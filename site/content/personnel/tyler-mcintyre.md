Tyler McIntyre graduated from Arizona State University with a B.S. in Accounting in December of 2010. Post graduation, Tyler spent five years as an associate at a Regional Accounting Firm in central Iowa. There, he gained valuable experience preparing financial statements, performing bookkeeping, and preparing tax returns for individuals and businesses. Outside of accounting, Tyler has held a wide variety of jobs…from teaching English in Japan, to working on a tourist boat in the Caribbean, to building rainforest trails in a tree house village in the jungles of Costa Rica! Tyler has since been domesticated and enjoys exercising, mowing the lawn, and bathtime with his new infant daughter.
