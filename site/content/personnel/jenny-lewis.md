Jenny Lewis graduated from the University of Utah with a Bachelors in Accounting and is a licensed CPA in the state of Virginia. She started her career at Ernst & Young, working in both Audit and Transaction Advisory Services before leaving public accounting to support small businesses in the government contracting space. She was a Controller for 10 years and then joined Pipaya (now Prosperity) in 2022. She recently moved from Virginia to Buffalo, NY, where she enjoys shoveling snow with her teenager.

**EXPERTISE:** Financial Due Diligence, Small Business Controllership, Financial Modeling and Forecasting
