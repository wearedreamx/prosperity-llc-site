Akshaya Moharana received a Master of Business Administration from Biju Patnaik University of Technology in Rourkela, Odisha in 2007. In February 2025, Akshaya joined Prosperity Partners. Prior to this, he worked at Thomson Reuters where he focused on reviewing individual tax returns. During his tenure at Thomson Reuters, Akshaya also spent time in Irvine, California supporting the team during both the busy and extension seasons. Additionally, he contributed to the development of the lead sheet in SP Binder, enhancing workflow efficiency.

**EXPERTISE:** Individual Tax Returns (Expat, Inpat, and Domestic)
