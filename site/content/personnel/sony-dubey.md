<p>Sony Dubey holds a Masters of Commerce from Mumbai University. Upon graduation, she began her career at Datamatics Global Services Ltd., where she laid the foundation for her journey in tax compliance. Sony started by preparing individual (1040) tax returns and quickly advanced her knowledge across multiple areas, including the partnership and corporate prep and review process. Over the years she has also taken on mentoring roles, guiding new team members, and contributing to a collaborative learning environment. During her three-year tenure at Thomson Reuters (SurePrep), she gained deep experience and comprehensive knowledge in all aspects of tax compliance. In March 2025, she joined Prosperity Partners, where her dedication, versatility, and commitment to continuous learning were quickly recognized with a promotion to her current role.</p>
