<p>Loise joined Prosperity Partners in January 2025 as an Administrative Assistant, overseeing the office administration and client billing for the Rockville team. Loise is originally from Kenya, where she pursued a Bachelor of Pharmacy at the University of Nairobi. After relocating to the United States, Loise transferred to Capella University and received a Bachelor of Public Health. Loise lives with her family in Rockville, Maryland, just a 5-minute drive from the Prosperity Partners office! Loise is fluent in three languages (English, Swahili and Kamba) with an intermediate knowledge of French. Before joining Prosperity Partners, Loise worked as an Administrative Assistant for the Henry M. Jackson Foundation (HJF), directly supporting the US Military HIV Research Program (MHRP) since 2022.</p>
