David Maenner is an executive leader with a proven track record of driving profitable growth in PE-backed companies. He specializes in transforming organizations for peak performance and profitability, using metrics and data-driven decision-making to identify and optimize potential. His experience across Operations, Finance, and Commercial functions has been instrumental in leading high-growth organizations.

For the last 15 years, David has worked within PE-backed portfolio companies, successfully leading and participating in strategic M&A transactions from diligence through post-merger integration. He is passionate about building and developing high-performing teams, fostering cross-functional collaboration, and driving continuous improvements to maximize organizational value. David has a B.S. from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and an MBA from DePaul University.
