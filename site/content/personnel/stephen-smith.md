<p>Steve Smith received a bachelor’s of science degree in accounting from California State University, Northridge.&nbsp; Steve spent five years at a local CPA firm in Oxnard, California, before going into private sector as a Chief Financial Officer of a local business to help bring the company out of financial difficulty. Upon successfully fulfilling that commitment, Steve decided public accounting was his love and joined McGowan Guntermann in 2008, becoming a partner in 2017.&nbsp; Steve works primarily in the areas of audit and review of not-for-profits and small businesses, small business accounting and consulting, and individual, partnership, not-for-profit, and corporate taxation and planning. Steve is a member of the California Society of Certified Public Accountants and the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants. Steve is currently on the Board of Directors and Treasurer of two non-profit organizations, the Miracle League of the 805 (ML805) and Camarillo Girls Softball Association (CGSA).&nbsp; Steve volunteers as a coach at various organizations, including AYSO, ML805 and CGSA.</p>
