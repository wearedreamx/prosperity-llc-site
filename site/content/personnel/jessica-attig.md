Jessica Attig graduated from the University of Vermont with a double concentration in Accounting and Finance. During her undergrad, she also spent time studying Finance at the University of Adelaide in Adelaide, Australia. She began an internship at Danaher Attig and Plante PLC during her senior year of high school and continued through college before joining the firm full-time! Jessica currently serves as a Supervising Senior Tax Associate with Prosperity Partners. Outside of work, Jessica enjoys spending time outdoors with her family and their golden retriever – whether it’s walking for ice cream or coffee, attending local concerts, or exploring farmers markets!

**EXPERTISE:** Individual and Business Tax Preparation & Review
